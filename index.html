What I work on

Research focus

⚡

Fast Calorimeter Simulation

Replacing Geant4 with deep generative models for hadronic shower simulation in ECal and HCal detectors, as point clouds with energy and time.

🌊

Diffusion & Flow Matching

Developing and benchmarking diffusion models and flow matching architectures for high-fidelity, geometry-independent shower generation.

🔁

One-Step Generators

Pushing the frontier of inference speed with latent mean-flow generators for pion showers — physics quality at near-zero latency.

📐

Geometry Independence

Training models that generalise across calorimeter geometries and particle types, reducing the cost of re-simulation for new detector designs.
